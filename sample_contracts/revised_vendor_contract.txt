MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE & PROPRIETARY RIGHTS AGREEMENT (REVISED DRAFT)

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on February 15, 2026, by and between Horizon Labs Inc. ("Party A") and Nexus Technologies Corp. ("Party B").

1. PURPOSE & EXCLUSIVITY
The parties wish to explore a potential strategic business collaboration regarding AI software integration. Party A agrees not to engage with any competing AI vendors during the evaluation period of six (6) months.

2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION DEFINITION
"Confidential Information" means all technical, business, financial, operational, and customer data disclosed by Party B, including all derivative works and concepts developed jointly during discussions.

3. EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIALITY
Information is excluded only if established by clear and convincing documentary proof that it was publicly known or previously possessed by Party A prior to disclosure.

4. OBLIGATIONS & RESIDUALS RESTRICTION
Party A agrees to hold Confidential Information in strictest confidence and shall not reverse engineer, decompile, or incorporate ideas into internal product lines. Party A waives any "residuals clause" protection for ideas retained in unaided human memory.

5. DURATION & SURVIVAL
The confidentiality obligations shall remain in effect for a period of five (5) years from disclosure, except trade secrets which shall remain protected in perpetuity.

6. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES & INJUNCTIVE RELIEF
In the event of an alleged breach of confidentiality by Party A, Party B shall be entitled to liquidated damages of $100,000 per violation without the requirement of proving actual damages, plus reimbursement of all legal counsel fees.

7. GOVERNING LAW & JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of New York State. Any dispute shall be adjudicated in the Commercial Division of the Supreme Court of New York.
