RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on March 1, 2026, by and between Oakridge Realty LLC ("Landlord") and Mark Taylor ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES & OCCUPANCY
Landlord hereby leases to Tenant the premises located at Unit 4B, 742 Evergreen Terrace, Springfield. The premises shall be occupied solely as a private residential dwelling by the named Tenant and no other persons.

2. TERM & RENT
The term shall commence on April 1, 2026, and expire on March 31, 2027. Monthly rent is $1,850.00, payable on or before the first day of each calendar month. A late fee of $75.00 shall automatically apply if payment is received after the fifth calendar day of the month.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $3,700.00 (equivalent to two months' rent) as security for full performance of lease terms. The deposit shall be returned within 30 days following lease expiration, subject to deductions for damages beyond ordinary wear and tear.

4. MAINTENANCE & REPAIRS
Tenant shall keep the premises in clean, sanitary order. Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs, plumbing systems, and electrical wiring. Tenant shall be responsible for minor repairs under $100 and routine HVAC filter replacements.

5. INSPECTION & RIGHT OF ENTRY
Landlord and authorized agents shall have the right to enter the premises for inspection, maintenance, or showing to prospective buyers upon providing a minimum of 24 hours advance written or electronic notice, except in cases of bona fide emergency.

6. SUBLETTING & ALTERATIONS
Tenant shall not assign, sublet, or transfer this lease, nor permit any portion of the premises to be utilized for short-term lodging (e.g., Airbnb) without prior written consent of Landlord. Tenant shall make no structural alterations without Landlord's written approval.

7. TERMINATION & RENEWAL NOTICE
Either party may terminate or request non-renewal of this Agreement upon serving at least 30 days advance written notice prior to the expiration date. In the absence of written notice, tenancy shall convert to month-to-month terms at a 10% rate increase.
